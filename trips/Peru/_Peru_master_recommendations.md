## 🗺️ Where to go (the core loop)

Almost every itinerary hits the same backbone: **Lima → Cusco / Machu Picchu →
Huacachina & Paracas**, with Arequipa and the Amazon as popular add-ons.

### Lima (2–3 days)
- Base yourself in **Miraflores** (Malecón boardwalk, **Parque del Amor**) and
  **Barranco** — repeatedly called everyone's *favorite neighborhood* (cafés,
  street art, **Puente de los Suspiros**, surf lessons).
- **Historic center:** Plaza Mayor / Plaza de Armas, the Cathedral, colonial
  palaces.
- **Hidden gems (via @filmthattv):** Inka Market (souvenirs, textiles),
  La Feria Union de Barranco, Parque Kennedy (the "cat park"), Larcomar
  cliffside mall, cross **Puente de la Paz** into Barranco.
- Known as one of the **best food cities** in South America — see food section.

### Cusco (4–6 nights) — the hub for Machu Picchu & the Sacred Valley
- **Take the first day easy to acclimate** — Cusco sits at ~11,000 ft.
- **First-day plan (via @inkatimeperu_engl):**
  1. Classic **city tour** — Qorikancha, Sacsayhuamán, Tambomachay, Puka Pukara
  2. **7 Borreguitos / "7 Snakes" street** — the most photogenic street
  3. **San Blas** neighborhood — artsy, peaceful, great coffee
  4. **Plaza de Armas** — cathedrals & colonial architecture
  5. **Cristo Blanco** — panoramic city + mountain views
- **Open/hop-on bus tour** of ~7 historic sites — only ~30 soles (@withashleyyy).
- Book a **Sacred Valley tour** that *ends in Ollantaytambo* so you can catch the
  train straight to Aguas Calientes (@daniel.bun_).

### Ollantaytambo / Sacred Valley (optional 2 nights)
- Quieter, nature-focused base ~2 hrs from Cusco; called a trip favorite
  (@elisetanriverdi).

### Paracas (1 day)
- **Ballestas / Palomino Islands boat tour** — penguins & sea lions (you can
  swim with sea lions). "One day is enough."

### Huacachina (1–2 nights) — desert oasis, a repeat crowd favorite
- **Dune buggies, sandboarding, sand tubing**, desert sunsets, **glamping**.
- **Duna Camp** glamping (@sydneycasonn): ~4 PM buggies → sunset → DJ, fire pits,
  meal, comfy tents. Local round-trip bus ~$15.
- Also: paragliding over the oasis, pool time, climb a dune for the overview.
- ~3 hr bus from Lima; store luggage with a service like **Bounce** (@jamie.fisch).

### Arequipa (3+ days) — "underrated"
- White-stone colonial architecture, volcano views; **Colca Canyon** and
  **Salt Flats** tours are the big draws (@withashleyyy).

### The Amazon (3–6 nights, add-on)
- Fly via Lima; **all-inclusive jungle lodges** with guide & meals included.

### Puno (Lake Titicaca) — mentioned as a northern/extra stop.

## ⛰️ Machu Picchu — how people do it

- **Book everything in advance** (entry + train), and pick a **morning slot** for
  better visibility (@traveltheworldwithdd, @traveltheworldwithdd).
- **Getting there:** Cusco → van → **Ollantaytambo** → train (e.g. **Inka Rail**)
  → **Aguas Calientes** (base town). Then **bus up**, or **hike up** (~1–1.5 hrs,
  "made it that much more rewarding").
- **Circuit 2 / 2A** is the classic route; **hire a guide** at the top.
- **Day trip vs. trek:**
  - Day trip from Cusco = cheapest/fastest (as little as **$45** entry).
  - **Salkantay Trek** (@_ontodaysadventure_): 50-mi, 5-day/4-night alternative to
    the Inca Trail (~$600 pp) — sky domes, jungle domes, cooked meals, mules
    carry packs, plus **Machu Picchu Mountain**.
  - Multi-day trails run 3–5 days — pad **1–2 acclimation days** in Cusco first.
  - Guided outfitter shout-out: **Alpaca Expeditions** (@bellaandherbackpack).

## 🌄 Bucket-list activities & day trips

- **Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca)** — ~16,000 ft, tough hike; go slow, bring
  altitude meds; add **Red Valley** on a clear day. Tours ~75 soles + entry,
  breakfast/lunch/guide included (@withashleyyy). Prefer not to hike? Take an
  **ATV up** (@traveltheworldwithdd).
- **Huacachina** dune buggies, sandboarding, sand tubing, paragliding, glamping.
- **Paracas** islands wildlife boat tour.
- **Llama Hotel** in the Andes — Peru-only stay, <£100/night (@traveltheworldwithdd).
- **Surf lessons** in Lima/Barranco to end the trip chill.

## 🍽️ Food & drink

- **Lima = top-tier food scene.** If you love seafood, **ceviche is a must**
  (also great in Barranco to finish the trip).
- **Cusco eats (@withashleyyy):**
  - **Kusi K'i** — best traditional Peruvian food, doesn't break the bank.
  - **Bar Den** — bar with a running lookout over the city.
- Larcomar & Parque Kennedy areas — lots of restaurants (Lima).

## 💡 Practical tips

- **Altitude is no joke** — acclimatize in Cusco before big hikes; bring altitude
  meds; go slow (repeated across creators).
- **Money:** carry **cash** for snacks, photo ops, and street-booked tours; you
  can book dune buggies/tours cheaply on the street or at hostels/bus stations
  rather than online (@bellaandherbackpack).
- **Tipping** is expected (less than the US, but for all services, not just
  Ubers).
- **Getting around:** **Uber** works well in cities; long-haul **buses** are the
  norm — **Cruz del Sur** named as a major line.
- **Respect locals:** ask (and pay) before photographing women with llamas.
- **Onward-ticket requirement:** you may need proof of onward travel to enter —
  services like **Onward Ticket** cover it (@bellaandherbackpack).
- **Book Machu Picchu + trains early**; morning entry = best views.
- **Luggage storage** (e.g. Bounce) for side-trips like Huacachina.

## 🧭 Sample itineraries people shared

**10 days (@daniel.bun_ / @withashleyyy):**
Lima (2–3d) → fly Cusco (acclimate) → Sacred Valley ending in Ollantaytambo →
train to Aguas Calientes → Machu Picchu (early) → Rainbow Mountain → chill day in
Cusco → back to Lima → Paracas + Huacachina → beach/surf to finish.

**2–3 weeks (@elisetanriverdi):**
Lima (2n) → bus to Paracas (1d, Ballestas) → Huacachina (2n, dunes) → back to Lima
→ fly Cusco (4–6n, Machu Picchu day trip) → Ollantaytambo (2n) → Amazon jungle
lodge (3–6n) → back to Lima.
