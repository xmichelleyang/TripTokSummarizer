I'm travelling with my fellow lesbian friend and content creator Allyssa, who's been to huacachina before and put me on. and I'm telling you guys, you cannot skip it if you're in Peru. after paying $15 for a local round trip bus, we checked into Duna Camp. it's this insane glamping experience that starts around 4PM with riding Dune buggies at full speed through the sand dunes. you also ride them to this crazy outlook area with views of Huacachina itself, which is the desert oasis, before heading further out into the dunes for sandboarding and my personal favorite, sand tubing. before heading to the camp, you stop for sunset in the dunes. and I don't know, you guys, there's something about a desert sunset that is so unique that everyone should experience at least once in their life. to be honest, I've done a lot of camping this summer, so I didn't expect much, but when we got to Duna Camp, I was blown away. it was so aesthetic. it had a DJ, fire pits, and a really delicious meal! and the tents themselves were actually really comfortable. when you book, you can choose to have a tent with one or two beds, and I genuinely had one of the best sleeps I've had in a long time. so save this for later when you're planning that Machu Picchu trip so that you don't forget to make a pit stop in the desert 🥰
