If you're visiting Lima, Peru, here are some hidden gems you can't miss. First up Inka Market. Looking for souvenirs? This is the spot. Tiny shops packed with everything Peruvian you can imagine textiles, crafts, trinkets. Great prices, friendly staff and perfect for bringing home a little piece of Peru. Next, La Feria Union de Barranco . They have small shows, kid friendly activities, local clothing brands and food carts. It's the perfect spot to relax and escape the hustle of Barranco And now the opposite of hidden gems -the touristy spots. They're popular for a reason. Plaza Mayor de Lima. The birthplace of Lima, surrounded by iconic palaces. Jump into history and don't skip the cathedral. The detail and architecture are breathtaking. Parque Kennedy. Chill with hundreds of friendly cats to befriend. Enjoy the vibe and check out the endless restaurants around. Larcormar, the famous cliffside shopping mall with amazing ocean views. Plenty of familiar U. S. stores and tasty bites. Then make your way across over the newly built bridge Puente de la Paz to get over to Barranco. Barranco Municipal Park, A lively family park with music and events. Hold your breath as you cross the Puente de los Suspiros and make your way down to the beach and enjoy the breeze.
