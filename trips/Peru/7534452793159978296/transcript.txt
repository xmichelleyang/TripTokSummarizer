I recently got back from spending 10 days in Peru and it was one of my favourite trips ever. So here's our itinerary and some of my favourite spots. We flew into Lima and spent one day here. We took the airport express shuttle into the city of Miraflores where we got to see this gorgeous lookout and also explore the park del Amor. The city also has an amazing food scene, so we ate as much as we could before flying out to Cusco the next day. Cuzco's altitude is much higher than Lima's, so we spent the first day just trying to acclimate. And we booked ourselves an open tour where they brought us around seven historic toric, scenic and beautiful spots around Cusco. We only paid 30 soles for this and I definitely recommend it if you're planning to visit Cusco. The next day, we booked our trip to Machu Picchu via Inka Rail. I booked the package on the Inka Rail website, which included the van pickup from Cusco to Olantaytambo and then the train from there to Awas Kilenti, which is also the base town of Machu Picchu. Once here, there is an option to take the bus up to Machu Picchu, but we decided to hike the trail up instead, which took about roughly an hour to an hour and a half. The views on the hike were incredible and it made Machu Picchu that much more rewarding. This was definitely a tiring day. So our third day in Cusco, we decided to take it chill and try to explore the food scene in Cusco. My favourite restaurant here was called Kusi K'i. They serve the most incredible traditional Peruvian food without breaking the bank. There are many beautiful bars around Cusco, but my favourite one yet was one that was called Bar Den, which had a running lookout over the city. Our fourth and last day in Cusco was another jam packed day. We woke up at 4:00am to hike to Rainbow Mountain. We booked this tour with one of the vendors in the main square at go and paid 75 soles each, not including the entrance ticket. They include breakfast and lunch in the package as well as a guide, which was such a good deal. We were so in awe the entire journey hiking up Rainbow Mountain and this was definitely one that we ticked off our bucket list. On our sixth day, we flew out from Cusco and headed to Arequipa, which I feel is such an underrated city in Peru. The town's architecture itself left me in awe and everywhere you look you see the volcanic ice capped mountains in the distance. We spent the first two days here just exploring the town and the main square. On our last day, we did a tour of the Ruta de LA Sierra which was used to form most of our aquipa's buildings. Also part of the tour was the Cabra De LA cuellabrea, which is this Canyon that formed from volcanic rock. I would definitely have loved to spend a few extra days in Arequipa because there are so many other tours that you can do, such as visiting the Salt Flats and also the Colca Canyon Tour, which are both very popular tours here in Arequipa. For our last day, we headed back to Lima and this time took the airport direct bus straight into the main square of Lima. We walked around here and saw all the activities that were happening in preparation for their Independence Day. And that was the end of our incredible trip.
