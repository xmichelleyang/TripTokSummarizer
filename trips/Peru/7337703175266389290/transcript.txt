Why did we pay almost $600 per person when it only cost $45 to visit Machu Picchu? That's because we chose to do the Sacantate Trek, a 50 mile trek similar to the incatrail, but not quite as popular. We spent five days in four nights hiking with an incredible group of strangers turned friends along the Andes Mountains. This included having Alex as our guide and mules who carried our packs along the way. We also had the same cook join us at each spot, so we had delicious home cooked meals at every break. And the best part was the accommodation along the way, like the Sky Dome where we could watch the stars all night, as well as the Indian huts and the jungle domes. This also included hiking Machu Pichu Mountain, one that most people don't get to experience but gives incredible views of Machu Pichu from above. But make sure to take the altitude seriously or you'll end up like Blake. Would you try the sock and tape track?
