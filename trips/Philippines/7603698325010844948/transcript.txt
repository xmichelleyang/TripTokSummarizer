Today, Mateus and I visited the Pukaway Caves in Coron, Philippines. The tour cost 1,500 pesos per person. This also included other stops like the Green Lagoon and coral reef. With the help of our guide and our safety equipment, it took us about 5 minutes to get down to the lake after climbing through a series of small caverns. Once in the cave, we were able to swim around with our snorkeling equipment. Overall, if you're looking for a hidden experience, I highly recommend the Pukaway Caves.
