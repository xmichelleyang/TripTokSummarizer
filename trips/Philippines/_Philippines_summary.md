10 had no caption track. Notes below use only the available transcripts.
Prices, travel times, and recommendations are creator-reported, not independently
verified or current quotes. Caption errors are flagged rather than silently corrected.

## Food & drink

- **Puning Hot Spring:** the creator says the day-tour package includes an
  unlimited buffet lunch with a wide selection. Lunch can be taken before or
  after the activities. No specific dishes or drinks are named. [Travelokaloka][puning]
- **El Nido boat tour:** the creator's combined Tour A/C included lunch during
  an approximately eight-hour outing. No menu or restaurant is named. [Sarcar][elnido]
- **Restaurants, cafes, bars, and local dishes:** none are specifically
  recommended in the available transcripts.

## Things to do

- **El Nido island hopping:** consider Tours A, B, C, and D. One creator combined
  A/C for Big Lagoon kayaking, then took a full-day Tour B for more caves,
  a sandbar stop, reefs, and snorkeling. They preferred B, while noting that
  A/C were also beautiful. Morgan Villas arranged their private tours.
  [Sarcar][elnido]
- **Coron snorkeling:** visit Kayangan Lake, Twin Lagoon, and shipwreck
  snorkeling sites. [Mikey][mikey]
- **Pukaway cave-lake swim:** a guide and safety equipment helped the creator
  descend through small caverns to an underground lake, taking about five
  minutes; they then swam with snorkeling equipment. Their tour also included
  Green Lagoon and a coral reef. [Nick][coron]
- **Puning day trip:** take a 4x4 ride between stations, soak in pools with
  different water temperatures at Station Three, then try the roughly
  15-20-minute sand treatment and volcanic-clay treatment at Station Two.
  [Travelokaloka][puning]
- **Kawasan Falls canyoneering:** a half-day experience with a zipline, hiking,
  scrambling, swimming, and jumps through three waterfall levels. The creator
  describes jumps up to 10 meters, says the jumps are optional, and also mentions
  a swing ride. [Annie][kawasan]
- **Other southern Cebu stops:** the itinerary mentions whale sharks at Oslob,
  plus the sardine run and sea-turtle snorkeling at Moalboal. These are mentions
  from the video, not an assessment of wildlife-tour operators. [Mikey][mikey]
- **Bohol sightseeing:** visit the Chocolate Hills, cruise the Loboc River,
  and relax on Panglao Island's beaches. [Mikey][mikey]
- **Makati spa stop:** Payapa offered the creator a 60-minute massage and
  30-minute facial massage, which they booked while waiting for Airbnb check-in.
  [Jackie][jackie]
- **Other activities in the budget roundup:** kayaking, island hopping,
  swimming among rock formations, a sardine swim, waterfall visits, a river
  zipline, and beach visits. Several location names are missing or mistranscribed,
  so this video alone is not enough to identify or book each stop.
  [Travel tips][budget]

## Tips / costs / logistics

- **Suggested route:** one creator proposes roughly two weeks starting in Manila,
  then Coron, El Nido, southern Cebu, an island whose name is captioned as
  "ciguay," and Bohol. Confirm that island name and transport connections before
  turning this into a booked itinerary. No day-by-day allocation is provided.
  [Mikey][mikey]
- **Arrival day:** allow time in Manila to rest, exchange money, and get a
  local SIM. [Mikey][mikey]
- **Advance bookings:** one creator recommends reserving domestic flights ahead
  of time, while booking other activities after arrival. Treat that as their
  experience, not an availability guarantee. [Poku][poku]
- **Private El Nido tours:** the creator says their private boats departed
  earlier than shared tours, helping avoid crowds, and allowed a more customized
  day. [Sarcar][elnido]
- **Pukaway tour price:** the creator reports **PHP 1,500 per person**, including
  the cave-lake visit, Green Lagoon, and a coral-reef stop. [Nick][coron]
- **Puning logistics:** the creator says day tours start at **8 a.m.**, there
  is no overnight option, reception has Wi-Fi despite weak mobile signal, and
  belongings can be left at the front desk. They describe a **20-30-minute**
  4x4 journey to Station Three. [Travelokaloka][puning]
- **What to bring to Puning:** sunscreen, comfortable clothing, and shoes.
  The creator says treatment clothing, soap, and towels are provided. Contact
  the operator for pricing, which the video says varies by season.
  [Travelokaloka][puning]
- **Kawasan costs:** captions state **"$162 per person"** without identifying the
  dollar currency; verify the amount before budgeting. The swing is quoted
  separately at **PHP 10 per ride**. [Annie][kawasan]
- **Payapa cost:** the creator reports **"$25" for 90 minutes** of massage and
  facial massage combined; the transcript does not identify the dollar currency.
  [Jackie][jackie]
- **Budget-roundup examples:** the captions quote **EUR 30** for a rock-formation
  swim, **EUR 3** for a sardine swim, **EUR 30** for island hopping,
  **EUR 4.80** for kayaking, **EUR 3 / EUR 0.50** for two waterfall visits,
  **EUR 10** for a river zipline, and **EUR 0.50** for a beach visit. Several
  destinations and other amounts are unclear; these are rough video claims,
  not reliable quotes for identified operators. [Travel tips][budget]
- **Mobile data:** the budget-roundup creator promotes an eSIM activated before
  departure. Its brand name and promotional terms are unclear in the captions,
  so no specific product recommendation is retained here. [Travel tips][budget]

## Transcript caveats

- The El Nido transcript renders a beach name as **"Antillou Beach"**; its
  claimed beach ranking is not verified here. [Sarcar][elnido]
- Poku's final island stop is inconsistently captioned as **"Shanghai"** and
  **"Sharga."** The video praises a coconut-tree viewpoint and the island's
  atmosphere, but the destination needs confirmation; it is not listed as
  Shanghai in these Philippines notes. [Poku][poku]
- Mikey's transcript renders one island as **"ciguay"** and another attraction
  as **"star shaped islands."** It describes the island's waterfalls, cliff
  jumping, snorkeling, and sunsets, but these unclear names should not be used
  for bookings without checking the video. [Mikey][mikey]
- Puning's treatment-related health claims are not adopted as medical advice.
  [Travelokaloka][puning]
