### Things to do
- First, start in Manila for a day to recover from your flight, exchange money, grab a local SIM card and experience a little bit of Filipino culture before heading out to the islands.
- This is one of the best places in the Philippines for island hopping, snorkeling, crystal clear water and incredible Limestone landscapes.
- Do not miss Kayangan Lake, Twin Lagoon, and the famous shipwreck snorkeling sites.
- Spend your days exploring hidden lagoons, secret beaches, towering Limestone cliffs, and some of the most stunning island hopping tours in Southeast Asia.
- Visit Oslob for whale sharks and head to moalboal to experience the world famous sardine run and sea turtle snorkeling.
