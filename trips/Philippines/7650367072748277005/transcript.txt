If you're planning your first trip to the Philippines, this is the exact two week itinerary I recommend to most travelers. First, start in Manila for a day to recover from your flight, exchange money, grab a local SIM card and experience a little bit of Filipino culture before heading out to the islands. From Manila, fly to Coron. This is one of the best places in the Philippines for island hopping, snorkeling, crystal clear water and incredible Limestone landscapes. Do not miss Kayangan Lake, Twin Lagoon, and the famous shipwreck snorkeling sites. Next, head to El Nido, often considered one of the most beautiful destinations in the Philippines. Spend your days exploring hidden lagoons, secret beaches, towering Limestone cliffs, and some of the most stunning island hopping tours in Southeast Asia. After Palawan, flight to Cebu and spend a couple days of exploring the south. Visit Oslob for whale sharks and head to moalboal to experience the world famous sardine run and sea turtle snorkeling. From Cebu, take a ferry to ciguay, one of the Philippines most underrated islands. Ciguay offers beautiful waterfalls, cliff jumping, snorkeling, laid back island vibes, and some of the most breathtaking sunsets in the country. Next, head to Bohol where you can visit the Chocolate Hills, see the famous star shaped islands, cruise the Loboc River, and relax on the white sand beaches of Panglao Island. This route gives you the perfect mix of beaches, island hopping, snorkeling, waterfalls, wildlife, adventure, and relaxation while keeping Travel logistics relatively easy.
