Craziest thing I've done in the Philippines was go canyoneering in Cebu at Kawasan Falls. This is a half day experience. It's only $162 per person and it starts with a zipline, then hiking, and then you get fully immersed in the water as you go jumping, scrambling and swimming through three different levels of Kawasan Falls. Now each jump gets progressively higher and higher until you get to the ultimate 10 meter jump. These jumps are optional, but it was the best part of the experience. There's also a swing ride here which cost 10 pesos extra each ride. Our group was large, so they assigned about 10 guides who took all the way to the end safe and sound. Highly recommend doing this if you're ever in Cebu. This will be the craziest experience of your life.
